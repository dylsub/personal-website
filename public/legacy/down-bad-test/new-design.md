# Down Bad Test: Claude's Version (2026)

Questions for evaluating how down bad a 22-25 year old is. Each question is tagged
`[good]` (checking it helps your score) or `[bad]` (checking it hurts your score).
The quiz page loads this file when "Take Claude's Version (2026)" is clicked, so
edits here show up on the page. Keep the `## Category` headings and the
`N. [good|bad] Question` format.

## Academics

1. [good] Graduated college on time (or are on track to)?
2. [good] Graduated (or will graduate) with a 3.5+ GPA?
3. [bad] Ever said "Cs get degrees" unironically?
4. [bad] Applied to fewer than 20 jobs/internships before graduating?
5. [good] Have a full-time job or grad program in the field you studied?
6. [good] Have a specific 5-year career plan you could explain right now?
7. [good] Got a promotion, raise, or return offer in the past year?
8. [good] Read a book you weren't assigned in the past 3 months?
9. [bad] Turned in AI-written work you didn't fully read?
10. [good] Learned a new professional skill or certification this past year?
