# Down Bad Test: Claude's Version (2026)

Questions for evaluating how down bad someone a few years out of college is.
Each question is tagged `[good]` (checking it helps your score) or `[bad]`
(checking it hurts your score). The quiz page loads this file when "Take
Claude's Version (2026)" is clicked, so edits here show up on the page. Keep the
`## Category` headings and the `N. [good|bad] Question` format.

## Career

1. [good] Have a full-time job with benefits?
2. [good] Make $70k+ a year?
3. [good] Make $100k+ a year?
4. [bad] Been unemployed for 6+ months since graduating?
5. [good] Got a promotion or a raise that beat inflation in the past year?
6. [bad] Is a college club still the top thing on your resume?
7. [good] Can you explain what you do at work in one sentence?
8. [bad] Spend most of your workday pretending to look busy?
9. [bad] Brought up your GPA or your college in a conversation this year?
10. [good] Have someone at work who would go out of their way to vouch for you?

## Health

11. [good] Work out or go to the gym 3+ times a week?
12. [good] Weigh about the same as (or less than) you did at graduation?
13. [bad] Hurt your back sleeping wrong at least once a month?
14. [good] Have a primary care doctor you've actually seen this year?
15. [bad] Eat lunch in front of a screen most days?
16. [bad] Order DoorDash/Uber Eats 3+ times a week?
17. [good] Cook most of your own dinners?
18. [bad] Drink alcohol 4+ nights a week?
19. [good] Get 7+ hours of sleep most nights?
20. [bad] Average 4+ hours of non-work screen time per day?

## Social
